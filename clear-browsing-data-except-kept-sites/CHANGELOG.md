## [1.0.1] - 2026-10-29

### New Features

* The extension now speaks your browser's language. It is translated into every language the Chrome Web Store supports and falls back to English for any other.
* The settings page, its warnings and dialogs, the report of the last clear, and the store description are all translated. Counts use each language's plural forms, dates follow its format, and the page is laid out right to left in Arabic, Hebrew and Persian.
