    Clearing

    The first clear can take several minutes when the browser holds a lot of browsing data. You can keep browsing in the meantime.

    Leftover Site Data

    Once the history and cookies of a site are deleted, the extension no longer finds it, so its local storage, databases and cache stay. Clear Leftovers deletes the site data of every site you do not keep, found or not, after a clear like Clear Now.

    Import and Export

    Save your kept sites to a text file, or replace them with the sites in one. A file can list one site per line or separate them with commas.
