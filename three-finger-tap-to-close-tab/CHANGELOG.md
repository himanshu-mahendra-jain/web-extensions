## [1.0.3] - 2026-10-17

### Changes

* Works on GooglebookOS.
* The Alt+Shift+W keyboard shortcut is removed. The browser's own Ctrl+W closes the current tab.
* Firefox needs version 153 or later, on the desktop and on Android.

### Bug Fixes

* Firefox for Android fixes.
* A web page can no longer close its own tab by faking a middle click.
