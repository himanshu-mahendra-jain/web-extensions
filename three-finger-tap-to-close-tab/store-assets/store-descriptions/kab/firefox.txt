Mdel iccer amiran s usiti s tlata iḍudan, s usiti alemmas neɣ s usiti deg ufeggag n yifecka.

Three Finger Tap to Close Tab d asiɣzef afessas, n uɣbalu yeldin, i d-yettmuddun abrid arurad d ufessas i usekkeṛ n yiccer amiran n yiminig.

Sit s tlata iḍudan neɣ sit s tqeffalt talemmast anda tebɣiḍ deg usebter web, beṛṛa n yiseɣwan d yiḥricen yettwaẓrigen, akken ad tmedleḍ iccer amiran. Tzemreḍ daɣen ad tsiteḍ ɣef tignit n usiɣzef deg ufeggag n yifecka.

Deg Firefox i Android, sit ɣef Three Finger Tap to Close Tab deg wumuɣ Isiɣzaf n yiminig akken ad tmedleḍ iccer amiran. Asiti ɣef ugdil mačči d asiti alemmas, ihi asiti ɣef usebter web ur yettmdal ara akk iccer.

Asiti alemmas ɣef yiseɣwan d tkarḍiwin n yisuddam yeṭṭef tikli tamagnut n yiminig, ihi tzemreḍ ad tkemmleḍ ad teldiḍ iseɣwan d yizen deg yiccaren imaynuten am wakken iwata. Talast n tɣellist i yellan deg-s tessewḥal amdal n yiccer s tuccḍa ma tsiteḍ ɣer tama n useɣwen neɣ n usenqed.

• Mdel iccer amiran s usiti s tlata iḍudan

• Ur yettmdal ara iccaren mi ara tesqedceḍ tiferkiwin d yiḥricen yettwaẓrigen
• Ulac umuɣen udhimen neɣ iɣewwaren

Tafelwit-ik n usenned d unagraw-ik n wammud ilaq ad ttwasbadun akken asiti s tlata iḍudan ad yefk asiti alemmas.
